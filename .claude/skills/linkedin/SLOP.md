# Slop patterns

The script catches word-level tells. These are the structural ones it cannot
see; check a draft against every one. A match is a finding only when it makes
the text vaguer or more generic: one em-dash is fine, a rhythm of them is slop.

## Structure

- **Reflexive contrast** — "It's not X. It's Y." / "This isn't about X, it's
  about Y." used to manufacture insight. Fix: state Y directly.
- **Rule of three** — lists of three abstract nouns or adjectives ("faster,
  safer, and more scalable") where one concrete fact would do.
- **Broetry** — every sentence its own paragraph, building to a reveal. Fix:
  merge into paragraphs of mixed length.
- **Summary instead of story** — lists what the project has, with no moment,
  no problem and no before/after.
- **Lesson inflation** — a small fix framed as a universal truth about
  engineering, leadership or life.
- **Symmetric sign-off** — a closing line that restates the hook, or a generic
  "What do you think?" / "Agree?".

## Content

- **Unfalsifiable claims** — "significantly faster", "much more reliable" with
  no number, unit or baseline.
- **Missing agent** — passive voice hiding who did it ("it was decided",
  "improvements were made").
- **Abstraction ladder stuck at the top** — "optimised the workflow" where the
  reader needs "moved classification into a pg-boss job".
- **Hedge stacks** — "arguably", "in many ways", "it could be said" piled up.
- **Fake vulnerability** — "I failed" that turns out to be a humblebrag; a real
  mistake names what broke and what it cost.
- **Borrowed authority** — "As experts say", "Studies show" with no source.

## Voice

- **Announcement register** — "thrilled", "humbled", "excited to share".
- **Hype adjectives** — cutting-edge, revolutionary, powerful, seamless,
  robust, game-changing, used without a fact behind them.
- **Emoji scaffolding** — emoji as bullets or section headers.
- **Uniform sentence length** — every sentence 12–18 words; real writing
  varies.
- **Over-hedged politeness** — "I'd love to hear your thoughts if you have a
  moment" style closers.
